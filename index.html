The Maucho Standard

A Boutique Approach to Global Export

We refuse to compromise quality for commercial mass-pooling. Our values define every shipment we send from Sri Lanka.

01

100% Purity, Guaranteed

We believe nature is perfect on its own. Our dehydrated fruits and herbal powders contain absolutely zero added refined sugars, chemical sulfur dioxide preservatives, artificial dyes, or masking agents. What your customers get is pure, concentrated Sri Lankan cultivation flavor.

02

Direct Estate Sourcing

By bypassing major commercial middlemen and corporate brokers, we secure our harvests directly from independent Sri Lankan farming families and ethical micro-cooperatives. This keeps our supply chain completely clean, transparent, and fair-trade.

03

Small-Batch Precision

Mass processing destroys the cellular structure and nutrient density of agricultural products. We process our exports in highly controlled, small batches using low-temperature technology to trap maximum vitamins, bright color, and premium organic aroma.

04

Eco-Minded Execution

Sustainability isn't a buzzword for us. We optimize our drying workflows to minimize fuel consumption, repurpose organic bi-product waste right back into agricultural compost, and work with bulk packaging configurations that aggressively cut down global transport footprints.
